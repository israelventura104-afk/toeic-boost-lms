# Part 1 Photographs — blueprint

**Official feel:** look at a photo → hear 4 statements → choose the best description.  
**Class bank target (Phase 1):** **40** items (10 per lote × 4 lotes), then grow toward ~100 for rich mixing.  
**Intro free:** 3 fixed items (`TQB-P1-0001`…`0003`).

## Scene mix (per 10-item lote)

| Scene type | ~count |
|------------|--------|
| Office / meeting | 3 |
| Service / retail / reception | 2 |
| Logistics / warehouse / travel | 2 |
| Outdoor / campus-adjacent workplace | 2 |
| Tech / lab / workshop | 1 |

## Distractor recipe (each item)

1. Wrong action (same people, different verb)  
2. Wrong subject / number of people  
3. Wrong object or location detail  

## Pipeline

1. Rubric + blueprint ✓  
2. Intro free (3) + practice UI  
3. Lotes → bank 40  
4. Guided (no timer)  
5. Timed mock (~6–10 Q)  
6. Strategies card on `strategies.html#part1`
